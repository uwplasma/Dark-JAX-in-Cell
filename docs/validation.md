![Resonant prescribed drive against an analytic cold forced oscillator](_static/figures/prescribed_drive/figure.png)

The prescribed drive is an external force: its growing resonant response is coherent, not an exponential instability, and no dark reservoir is depleted. Its [run record](_static/figures/prescribed_drive/run.json) includes the accumulated work.

![Matched seeded zero-drive and homogeneous-drive controls](_static/figures/homogeneous_null/figure.png)

In the restricted nonrelativistic electron-only electrostatic model with a uniform fixed neutralizer, a spatially uniform drive can be removed by an accelerating-frame transformation. The matched [null-control run](_static/figures/homogeneous_null/run.json) compares the magnitude of one seeded nonzero-$k$ electric mode. Its small but not yet clearly convergent difference rules out a large effect in this setup; it does **not** reproduce a mobile-ion nonlinear instability or establish a numerical convergence rate.

The [Hook–Huang–Shalaby simulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13956v1) uses mobile electrons and ions, a 40 $c/\omega_p$ box with 1,000 cells, fifth-order interpolation, and long resonant pumping. Our homogeneous null uses a fixed neutralizer and the parent's quadratic deposition; the published nonlinear heating and saturation curves have **not** been reproduced. The prescribed-drive mode is an imposed external reservoir. A self-consistent finite dark packet is studied separately in the [slab experiment](profile.md), with a different transverse propagation observable.

![Oblique magnetized cold 3V response](_static/figures/oblique_3v/figure.png)

The oblique-field figure compares all six mean electric-field components with an independent cold-fluid matrix. Its [run record](_static/figures/oblique_3v/run.json) retains the magnetic-vector and resolution settings. It exercises all three particle velocities, but does not establish a warm kinetic instability rate.

The current implementation supports periodic explicit electromagnetic Ampere PIC without filters, collisions or sources. Initial dark arrays supplied by a caller must satisfy dark Gauss. The SI rest frequency is positive; a massless gauge limit, absorbing Proca boundaries, dark-charged matter and openPMD dark export are outside this release. Native NPZ restart is complete for supported runs. Do not interpret the mean homogeneous ordinary field as radiated photon power, and do not interpret a prescribed drive as a depleted dark reservoir.

Eight isolated development-only fault injections—removing the dark force/source, reversing the Proca mass sign, removing the mean current, zeroing the scalar potential, dropping potential energy, breaking the shared neutralizer, and resetting the drive clock—each caused its focused physics or restart test to fail. The production source was untouched; [the fault list and results](_static/figures/defect_controls.json) record the exact substitutions and test names.
