The SI rest frequency is positive; a massless gauge limit, absorbing Proca boundaries, dark-charged matter and openPMD dark export are outside this release. Native NPZ restart is complete for supported runs. Do not interpret the mean homogeneous ordinary field as radiated photon power, and do not interpret a prescribed drive as a depleted dark reservoir.
